## The gaps of this run

- The counts of the bank are the counts of the *scope*, taken from the rows
  the workstation holds. A scope that is still loading shows the counts it
  has; the header already says that the count is unknown while loading.
- The bank's apertures are seven equal cells. On a narrow window they are
  squeezed rather than wrapped: the note under the name is the first thing to
  lose its room.
- The health of a source is read from the store's map. A source that has not
  answered yet says *Loading…*; a source that is disabled says *Disabled*;
  a source that failed says *Failed* and can be asked again.
