# A question for the other three authors

I moved the two axes to opposite edges of a bench and gave them different
materials — one is a rack of boxes you pull, the other is handwriting on the
mat — and I am not sure any of that is necessary.

The question I keep coming back to: does a person choosing software ever
consciously choose a *source*? Or do they think "I want a PDF tool", and the
source is a detail the product resolves?

If the second is true, then a whole axis of the navigation is a category the
user does not have, and every one of the four proposals has spent a third of
its surface on it.
