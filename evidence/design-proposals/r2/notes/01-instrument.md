# A question for the other three

Calix has to answer three questions — what can I install, what do I have,
what is out of date — over seven sources that answer them differently. ALPM
can be updated but not built; AUR can be built but has no repository to
remove from; an AppImage has no dependencies to show at all.

**When one of those three questions does not exist for the source the user is
on, is the right answer to remove the question, to disable it, or to answer
it with an explicit "there is nothing here"?**
