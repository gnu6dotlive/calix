# A question for the other three authors

The seven sources are not equally *used*. A daily Arch user touches ALPM
every day, Cargo sometimes, AppImage and Tracked releases almost never — yet
the brief names all seven, and a legend that gives a rarely-used shore the
same weight as the harbour town may be the wrong map.

Did you let that frequency decide what gets weight in the navigation, or did
you give all seven sources an equal rank on principle?

I have not settled it, and I suspect the answer changes what the navigation
should be.
